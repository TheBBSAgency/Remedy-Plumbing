The Origin Story

Built around honest service — not chasing emergencies.

Remedy's roots run deep in Fond du Lac. Our founder grew up around the trade — his dad ran a local plumbing company in the 1990s called Affordable Plumbing. He started his own apprenticeship alongside his brother in 2003 and never looked back.

By 2016, after years of being the one other plumbers called when they couldn't figure something out, he founded Remedy Plumbing. The name came after about a month of late nights trying to settle on what to call the company. The crew had always been the ones who could troubleshoot the tough problems — they always had the remedy. The name clicked.

The mission was simple: build a plumbing company where the work gets done right, the customer gets treated well, and the crew gets home in time for parent-teacher conferences. Ten years later, that's still how Remedy runs.

Why Work-Life Balance

Why we don't answer the phone at 2am.

The plumbing trade has a culture of always-on service: 24/7 emergency lines, plumbers crawling out of bed at 3am for calls that often turn out to be non-emergencies the customer didn't want to pay extra for.

Remedy tried that for the first four years. After a hundred middle-of-the-night calls that turned into exactly two paying jobs, the math stopped working — for the company and for the families at home.

So Remedy runs Monday through Friday, 7am to 5pm. It means we miss some calls. It also means our team is rested, present at home, and not burned out. The customers who matter most — the ones who become repeat clients and refer their neighbors — value that consistency over after-hours availability.
